## Data and deleted support

- `xai_http_inputs.json` retains 94 historical client inputs and scripted peer replies. It contains no recorded upstream request, chosen URL, downstream output, chunks, error, or usage expectations.
- `xai_oauth_inputs.json` retains 26 login scripts and 7 refresh scripts. Two pure parser-coercion cases were removed: `token_keys_fold_and_last_duplicate_wins` and `numeric_refresh_token_and_bool_base_url`. Typed-field validation replaces their oracle.
- `meta_peer_inputs.json` retains 36 client/credential/peer scenarios. Writer/decoder cases use direct Rust tables instead of fixtures.
- Deleted the Meta `device_fixtures` tree, including `TestRSFixMeta`, `TestRSFixMetaLogin`, `TestRSFixMetaWriter`, `TestRSFixMetaDecode`, capture/frame utility support and old output fixtures.
- Deleted xAI HTTP, OAuth and filename Go fixtures and their generators, plus proxy and multipart-form Go fixtures and the multipart-form generator.
- Deleted unused test-side Go request comparisons, usage-record comparisons, log text renderers and fixture loaders from `device_fixture.rs`. Local peers and typed observers remain.
- Deleted unused `controls_testkit.rs` and its module declaration, plus `payload_barrier::with_v8_0_35` after all consumers migrated.
- The inactive Claude-directory fixtures `go_claude_device_pool_home.json` and `go_xai_replay_home.json` have no remaining consumers in this scope; their parent agent owns deletion. xAI WebSocket/URL generator cleanup belongs to the Codex agent.

## Production change

xAI OAuth expiry uses checked duration construction and timestamp addition. Unrepresentable positive token expiries become unknown, rather than wrapping. Positive device-flow lifetime caps at 30 minutes before conversion; huge values no longer wrap into immediate expiry. Poll intervals use checked positive seconds and the same cap. No provider traffic was sent.
